About Me

I am an Eagle Scout who graduated as valedictorian from Nevada State High School. During my high school years, I completed two years of dual credit enrollment at the College of Southern Nevada while also working as a part-time lifeguard for the City of Henderson—balancing academics, work, and community service.

I served a full-time two-year proselyting mission for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints in the Arkansas Little Rock Mission, where I developed leadership skills, cultural awareness, and fluency in Spanish while serving Spanish-speaking communities.

Now, I am focused on my undergraduate degree in Accounting at Utah State University's Huntsman School of Business. I am driven by a passion for financial integrity, attention to detail, and using my analytical skills to make a meaningful impact in the business world.
